A talent fellowship through autumn 2026, with a one-week sprint in San Francisco, 26 October – 1 November. All expenses covered.

Questions

We're looking for people who want to make meaningful changes happen with their time. High-agency builders, hustlers and big thinkers. It is extremely helpful to be highly technical or exceptional in some way. This likely means you have some interesting experience with personal projects that are technical and/or somehow monetizable. Two cohorts so far — between them: international science olympiad medallists, national competition winners, researchers publishing at the top of their age class, founders who had built and sold a company before 21, and open-source authors with libraries used by thousands.
You do not need past personal projects to apply but we want to somehow see that you are able to meaningfully progress something you care about. We appreciate complex products equally to quickly put together prototypes as long as you have the hunger to develop them much further. Even small-scale or unfinished projects are interesting but be sure to describe where they are headed and why you haven't yet gotten there.
No. We are looking for young people with a bright future rather than a bright past.
To apply you need to be a Finnish citizen or permanent resident and be under 25 years old.
26 October – 1 November 2026. Applications are open 7–21 September, decisions by 28 September.
Participation for chosen fellows is free. Travel and accommodation are covered by the Technology Industries of Finland Centennial Foundation. You only cover your personal expenses during the trip.
A valid passport (expiry no earlier than May 2027) and an approved ESTA. With four weeks between the decision and the flight, these cannot wait.
Mention it in the application and be prepared to put extra effort into securing highly relevant meetings for yourself. We're very open to participants following their own agenda for a valuable outcome.
