About Byte

Byte is a talent fellowship that takes young, technically talented people from Finland to San Francisco for a week of conversations with founders, builders and investors. The group is small on purpose, so every meeting is personal.

Byte 3 is the third batch. It is supported by the Technology Industries of Finland Centennial Foundation. Learn more at joinbyte.so.
